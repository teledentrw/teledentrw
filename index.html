About TeleDent Rwanda

TeleDent Rwanda is an innovative tele-dentistry platform designed to improve access to oral healthcare across Rwanda. Patients can connect with licensed dentists, receive professional guidance, upload photos, schedule appointments and manage treatment records through a secure digital platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TeleDent Rwanda?

TeleDent Rwanda is a tele-dentistry platform connecting patients with licensed dentists.

How do online consultations work?

Patients upload symptoms and dental photos which are reviewed by professional dentists.

Can I choose a dentist?

Yes, patients can view dentist profiles and choose approved professionals.

How are payments made?

Consultation payments can be completed through supported digital payment channels.

Can dentists register?

Yes. Dentists register and are approved before joining the public directory.

Does TeleDent Rwanda support education?

Yes. Academic resources and oral health education content are available through the Academics section.
