About Teledent Rwanda

Welcome to TeleDent Rwanda, Your Partner in Modern Dental Care

TeleDent Rwanda is a digital oral healthcare platform designed to bridge the gap between patients and licensed dental professionals. Our platform enables users to receive online consultations, upload dental images, obtain professional diagnoses, schedule appointments and access oral health education resources. By combining healthcare and technology, TeleDent Rwanda aims to make quality dental services accessible across Rwanda.

Our mission is to revolutionize the dental industry by leveraging technology to enhance the patient experience. Whether you need a routine check-up, have oral health concerns, or seek cosmetic dentistry evaluations, Dentulu has you covered. We offer second opinions from trusted dentists, specialist referrals, and even AI-assisted guidance for comprehensive care.
